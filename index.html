🔒 Locked In

Optional

When a prayer starts, Noor opens a prayer screen and pins your phone to it, so you put the phone down and pray. Fajr is never locked.

Turn on Locked InLocks at Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha

Prayers to lock
Prayer notificationsA notification at all 5 prayers, even when the app is closed
On iPhone, Locked In opens the prayer screen when Noor is open at prayer time. For a real lock, use Guided Access: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → turn it on. When the prayer screen appears, triple-click the side button and tap Start. Your phone stays on Noor until you triple-click again and enter your passcode.
For the full lock, turn on Pin app on your Samsung: Settings → Security and privacy → More security settings → Pin app. Without it, Locked In still shows the prayer screen and alert. Open Noor at least once a day so the alerts stay scheduled.
